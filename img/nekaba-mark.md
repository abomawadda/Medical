# Union mark presentation

`nekaba-mark.png` is the transparent, high-resolution restoration prepared with the image editing tool from the owner's existing `nekaba_logo.png` on 2026-10-18. The original file remains preserved. The restored version is used on startup, login, and the personal card with `object-fit: contain`, without circular clipping or multiply blending. It is a presentation asset, not an official replacement of the union's master artwork.
